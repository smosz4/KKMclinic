Our Medical Services

General Medicine

Comprehensive medical care for adults including diagnosis, treatment, and management of common illnesses and chronic conditions.

Laboratory Services

Full range of diagnostic tests including blood work, urinalysis, cultures, and pathology services with accurate, timely results.

Minor Surgery

Outpatient surgical procedures performed with precision and care, including wound repairs, cyst removals, and biopsies.

Antenatal / Maternity Care

Complete prenatal, delivery, and postnatal care ensuring the health of both mother and baby throughout pregnancy.

Dental Care

Comprehensive dental services including examinations, cleanings, fillings, extractions, and oral health education.

HIV / AIDS Testing & Counselling

Confidential testing, counseling, and support services with compassionate care and complete privacy protection.

Paediatrics

Specialized medical care for infants, children, and adolescents including immunizations, growth monitoring, and illness treatment.

Ultrasound Scan

Advanced diagnostic imaging for pregnancy monitoring, abdominal examinations, and other medical assessments.

Pressure Check-up

Regular blood pressure monitoring, hypertension management, and cardiovascular health assessments.

About KKM People's Clinic

Our Mission

At KKM People's Clinic, we are committed to providing accessible, high-quality healthcare to individuals and families in our community. Our patient-centered approach ensures personalized care tailored to each individual's unique health needs.

Our Values

  • Compassionate Care
  • Medical Excellence
  • Patient Privacy
  • Community Focus

Our Medical Team

Our clinic is staffed by experienced, board-certified physicians, nurses, and healthcare professionals dedicated to providing the highest standard of medical care. We continuously update our knowledge and skills through ongoing medical education.

We utilize modern medical equipment and follow evidence-based practices to ensure accurate diagnoses and effective treatments for all our patients.
